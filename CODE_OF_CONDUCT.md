# Code of Conduct

ResilientMQ follows the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free community, regardless
of identity, background, experience or ability. Constructive technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation and publication of
private information are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing work.
- Assume good intent while still taking responsibility for impact.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and the change being considered.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect requests to stop unwelcome interaction.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@didactika.org`. Reports are reviewed by
the Didactika maintainers responsible for ResilientMQ. They may remove content, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban someone according to the
severity and recurrence of the behavior.

The maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible. Deliberately
false or retaliatory reports are themselves a violation of this policy.
